Support

Juicy Sudoku — classic sudoku played with fruits instead of numbers, and no ads.

Write to us: tritonbios@gmail.com

We answer in English, Russian and Ukrainian, usually within two days. In the game, Settings → “Write to us” adds your app version automatically.

How do I get Premium back on a new iPhone or iPad?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open Juicy Sudoku and tap “Restore purchases” at the bottom of the Premium screen or in Settings. Premium is a one-time purchase and never needs to be bought again.

Can my family use Premium?

Yes. Premium supports Family Sharing: members of your Apple family group get it for free. They open the Premium screen and tap “Restore purchases”.

How do I play with numbers instead of fruits, or make them bigger?

Open Settings in the game. “Symbols” switches between fruits, animals, shapes, letters, runes and numbers; in a game, the round button with the fruit next to Settings does the same. “Digit size” changes the symbols on the board, “Text size” changes the menus and buttons. The game also follows the text size set in iPhone Settings → Display & Brightness → Text Size.

Is my game saved?

Yes, automatically after every move. You can close the app at any time and continue later, even without internet.

Are there ads? Is there a subscription?

No ads in any version, ever. The free version is a complete game. Premium is a single payment, not a subscription.

Does the game collect data about me?

No. There is no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your games, statistics and medals stay on your device. Purchases are handled by Apple.

How do I ask for a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
